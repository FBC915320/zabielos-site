Product development and Private Label

We assist product specifications and the search for factories capable of customization, packaging and private-label production, subject to feasibility and agreement.

Practical example

A wholesaler wants cookies and savory snacks under its own brand. We research factories, packaging options and minimum order quantities.

How we can assist

  • Define product and packaging requirements
  • Find potential contract manufacturers
  • Assess packaging, labeling and order minimums
  • Support sample and proposal coordination

Information we need

Product type, materials or ingredients, packaging, brand, estimated volume, sales country and regulatory needs.

Commercial arrangement

Product-development fees or contractual commission.

Scope and terms are agreed per project. Customs clearance, certification, inspections and freight transport are not presented as in-house services; qualified third parties may be required.
