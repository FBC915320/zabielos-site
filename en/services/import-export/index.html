Import and export

We assist the commercial planning of cross-border sourcing and sales, working alongside specialized service providers as needed.

Practical example

A Brazilian distributor wants products from Paraguay. We research suppliers, compare offers and coordinate commercially with logistics and customs professionals.

How we can assist

  • Research products, suppliers and buyers
  • Compare quotations and commercial terms
  • Support negotiations on volumes, deadlines and applicable Incoterms
  • Coordinate with third-party customs and logistics specialists

Information we need

Product, origin, destination, volume, requirements and intended transaction structure.

Commercial arrangement

Project fees or an agreed commercial commission.

Scope and terms are agreed per project. Customs clearance, certification, inspections and freight transport are not presented as in-house services; qualified third parties may be required.
