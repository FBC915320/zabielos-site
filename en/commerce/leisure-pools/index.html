Leisure and pools

Swimming pool protection covers

Polyethylene sheets for protecting pools from leaves, dust and dirt. Custom sizing and fittings subject to quotation.

Pool safety covers

Reinforced PVC covers and fastening systems according to dimensions and technical requirements.

Thermal pool covers

Bubble-texture thermal blankets designed to help retain water temperature and reduce evaporation.

Gazebos and outdoor protection

Pop-up gazebos and side panels for leisure, events, gardens and outdoor areas.

Applications and buyers

  • Residential pools, clubs and condominiums
  • Pool product retailers and distributors
  • Gardens, events and recreation areas
  • Distributors of outdoor covers and accessories

Quotation requirements

Specify product category, dimensions, shape, material, thickness, color, accessories, quantities, destination and intended use.

Thermal, protection and safety covers serve different purposes. Do not assume load-bearing or fall-protection capacity without technical verification and applicable certifications.

Quotation requirements

Genuine product photography without logos. Pricing, supply and terms require confirmation.
